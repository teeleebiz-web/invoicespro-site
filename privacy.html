Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how InvoicePro and BizHQ ("we," "our," or "us") collect, use, and protect your information when you use our apps and website. We keep this in plain language, because you deserve to actually understand it.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you create an account and use the app, including your name, email address, business details, and the client, invoice, proposal, contract, and document information you enter. We also collect basic usage information to keep the app running smoothly.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide the service — creating and sending invoices, managing clients, generating proposals and contracts, processing payments, sending reminders and receipts, and powering the AI assistant. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted providers to deliver parts of the service:

  • Supabase — secure database and account storage.
  • Stripe — payment processing. Your payment card details are handled directly by Stripe and never stored on our servers.
  • Resend — sending emails such as reminders and receipts.
  • Anthropic — powering the AI assistant.
  • BoldSign — electronic signatures on contracts.

Each provider handles your data under their own security and privacy standards.

AI Data Handling

When you use the AI assistant, the relevant business information needed to answer your request is processed to generate a response. This is used only to serve your request — not to train outside models on your private business data.

Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, your data is retained for 30 days and then permanently deleted.

Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your information at any time from within the app. If you need help, contact us and we will assist you.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at support@invoicespro.org.
