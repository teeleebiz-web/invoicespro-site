Our story

Built for the person doing the work—and everything around it.

InvoicesPro began with a simple belief: being a small business should not mean settling for tools that are too complicated, too expensive, or designed for somebody else's way of working.

01

What we saw

The smallest businesses carry the widest jobs.

The independent professional may be the craftsperson, salesperson, project manager, customer service desk, and bookkeeper—all in the same afternoon. Yet much of business software assumes there is a team behind the screen.

That gap is what our products are designed to close.

02

What we believe

Professional should not mean complicated.

A strong tool can be capable without being intimidating. It can use plain language, show the next step, and help someone present their business with confidence—without turning ordinary work into software training.

03

What we are building

A focused product first. A connected headquarters next.

InvoicePro begins with one of the clearest business needs: create the invoice, make payment easy, and know what happened. BizHQ expands that philosophy across the rest of the client relationship.

The two products share a purpose, but they do not force every customer into the same system.

How we decide

Four principles behind every screen.

01

Plain language

Use the words people already use when they talk about their work.

02

Respect the time

Make common actions direct and keep unnecessary steps out of the way.

03

Show what matters

Bring money, clients, and next actions forward before secondary detail.

04

Keep people in control

Use automation to assist judgment, not quietly replace it.

“Made for the person who is the whole company.”
