What it does

Everything around the invoice—without everything else.

InvoicePro stays intentionally focused, giving independent professionals the payment tools they need most.

01

Fast invoicing

Add the client, amount, service, and invoice date without navigating an accounting system.

02

Stripe payment links

Create a direct online payment path for each standard invoice or installment.

03

Installment plans

Split a balance into weekly, biweekly, or monthly payments and track each one.

04

QR payments

Generate a scannable payment code for in-person work, events, and on-site services.

05

Status at a glance

See paid, pending, overdue, and archived invoices without digging through records.

06

Professional exports

Download an invoice as a PDF or export selected invoice history for your records.
