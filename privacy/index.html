Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

InvoicePro is a mobile invoicing application operated by Terrence Lee, a sole proprietor based in California. This policy explains how InvoicesPro handles information across InvoicePro, BizHQ, and this website.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide when you create an account, including your name, business name, email address, and password. When you use InvoicePro, we also process invoice information such as client names, client contact details, service descriptions, and payment amounts. Basic technical and usage data may be collected to operate and improve the service.

How we use information

We use information to operate the apps and website, authenticate accounts, create invoices, generate payment links, process requested actions, send transactional messages, provide support, and improve product reliability. We do not sell personal information and do not use invoice data for third-party advertising.

Service providers

Stripe is used to process payments and create payment links. Transactional email services may be used to deliver receipts and account messages. These providers receive information only as needed to perform the service requested.

Client information

A business user may enter information about their own clients. The business user is responsible for having an appropriate reason to provide and use that information, including consent for optional SMS notifications where applicable.

Data retention and deletion

Account and business data is retained while an account is active or as needed to provide the service. InvoicePro includes an account-deactivation request with a 30-day recovery window before permanent deletion is intended to occur. Contact support for help with a deletion request.

California privacy rights

California residents may request information about personal data we collect or request deletion, subject to applicable exceptions. We do not sell personal information. Requests can be sent to support@invoicespro.org.

Age requirement

The products are intended for people 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the products change. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@invoicespro.org.
